Description

Take A Break Games is a two-person studio dedicated to making polished, thoughtfully crafted games. Tom Yancey and Jared Von Nickerl split the work between them, drawing on their individual strengths in programming, illustration, animation, user experience, and game design. Our first game, WyrmWorm, is a grid-filling logic puzzle built in Godot and headed to Steam.

History

How we started

WyrmWorm began as a game jam entry in January 2026. We had just come off months of grinding on a larger project and wanted something with a more focused scope that we could finish and polish properly. The jam build was fun to make and well received, so we decided to keep going and turn it into a full game. Over the following months, WyrmWorm gained new mechanics like portals, a major visual overhaul, and a world map, growing into a cohesive, polished puzzle game. Take A Break Games formed around the project in May 2026.

Going full time

In 2025 Jared left a full-time career in illustration and concept art (including card art for Disney Lorcana and NDA board game work) to make games full time. Tom is a Software Engineer who wanted to use his skills for bringing joy directly to players, and decided to change game development from a hobby to a full-time job. WyrmWorm has been in development since January 2026, with a public playtest on Steam as of October 2026.
