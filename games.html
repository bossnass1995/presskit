Description

Take A Break Games is a two-person studio making hand-painted puzzle games. Tom Yancey handles code, systems, and music; Jared Von Nickerl, a former illustrator and concept artist, handles art, animation, and design. Our first game, WyrmWorm, is a grid-filling logic puzzle built in Godot and headed to Steam.

History

How we started

WyrmWorm began as a game jam entry in January 2026. Jared and Tom had just come off months of grinding on a larger project and wanted something small enough to finish and polish properly. The jam build was fun to make and fun to play, so they kept going. Jared wanted hand-painted art, Tom wanted puzzle mechanics worth building on, and the first ideas, hydras and time travel, grew into the branching wyrms and the fill-every-cell rule. Take A Break Games formed around the project in May 2026.

Going full time

In 2025 Jared left a full-time career in illustration and concept art (including card art for Disney Lorcana and NDA board game work) to make games full time. WyrmWorm has been in development since January 2026, with a public playtest on Steam as of October 2026.
